# Structural Babel Detection

Structural Babel Detection is a deterministic audit layer for detecting potential Babel-shaped structural risk in delegated AI systems.

It does not approve, block, or execute actions. It produces human-reviewable Babel Risk Reports that ask whether capability and coordination are scaling faster than agency, discernment, authority clarity, accountability, and proof.

It also includes Governance Absorption Capacity / Babel Velocity v0.1 for temporal analysis of whether quality-weighted governance closure is keeping pace with consequence-weighted agent decision throughput.

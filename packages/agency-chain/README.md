# AGS Agency Chain Mapper

Terminology: Alignment Governance Stack (AGS). See the [canonical architectural concepts](../../docs/MODULAR_ARCHITECTURE.md#core-architectural-concepts).

Deterministic local mapping for delegated agentic workflows.

The Agency Chain Mapper identifies where human or organizational agency is demonstrated, weakened, bypassed, or not demonstrated across policy, delegation, approval, runtime binding, execution boundary, receipts, and governance memory.

It does not determine moral responsibility, legal compliance, or wrongdoing.
